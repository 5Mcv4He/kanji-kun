## Implications for Learning Strategy

1. **You already know the meaning** of most JLPT words — focus on pronunciation
2. **On-yomi** words are easier (similar to Chinese readings) — learn kun-yomi rules for the rest
3. **Single-kanji words** are the hardest (purely Japanese readings) — use radical/component rules
4. **Compound words** are mostly on+on at higher levels — kun-yomi matters most at N5/N4
